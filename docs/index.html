Normalization is part of the derivative

For raw weights w with total W, normalized a=w/W and da=(dw-a·sum(dw))/W. A direction proportional to the whole raw vector changes no normalized mass. Zero-weight rows stay excluded; no derivative across changing support is claimed.

Inspect each cell and each constraint

Log mass is retained even where a positive active plan entry underflows to zero. Its displayed floating tangent can underflow too. Excluded cells have null log mass, not a fabricated log zero.

Every recentered potential and derivative

The gauge convention subtracts the mean row log scaling and adds it to the column scaling after every full update. The dual convention is f=epsilon·u, g=epsilon·(v+1), matching F=ΣPC+epsilon·ΣP log(P).
